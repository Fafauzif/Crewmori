Design a high-fidelity **desktop web application frontend** called **Principal**. This is a visual exploration with fictional data. Generate the main business workspace first, then connected screens using the same design system. Do not build a landing page or require a backend, credentials or external integrations.

## Product and purpose

Principal gives a freelancer a visible AI business team. The team helps find suitable international clients, promote services, handle English conversations, manage relationships, coordinate capacity and track payment. The freelancer performs the actual commissioned creative or technical work.

Our owner is **Alya Putri**, an Indonesian motion designer. She wants another US client. Her six cute specialist agents collaborate in a shared workspace:

- **Scout:** opportunity discovery and portfolio-fit assessment.
- **Promoter:** community posts and service campaigns.
- **Sales:** English buyer voice/text, qualification and offer discussion.
- **Account Manager:** client history, follow-ups and repeat-work opportunities.
- **Operations:** availability, deadlines, onboarding and buyer inputs.
- **Finance:** pricing, deposits, payment conditions and receipts.

Make the six-agent team a defining part of the interface. Give every agent a distinct original rounded character, role color, name, status and current task. Use restrained vector-like characters with expressive eyes and small work accessories. Keep the business interface polished and readable.

## Visual direction

Warm ivory canvas, off-white surfaces, dark ink text and forest-green primary actions. Small apricot, coral, blue, lilac, sage and mint accents identify individual agents. Use generous spacing, fine borders, gentle shadows and rounded geometry. Choose Manrope for headings and Inter for body text, with sensible sans-serif fallbacks. Use tabular numerals for money.

The overall feeling should be welcoming, capable and alive. The user should want to open this workspace every morning. Avoid a generic analytics template, excessive metric cards, neon/cyberpunk decoration, giant gradients or a full-screen chatbot. Characters belong beside useful work. Preserve clear buttons, hierarchy and legible text.

## Agent inspector and connected screens

The inspector shows the selected character, current task, inputs, concise action history, resulting artifact and controls: Message, Pause, Cancel. Give it an editable instruction field. Show sources and outputs, not internal reasoning or technical logs.

After Screen A, generate these connected views:

- **B. Opportunity assessment:** buyer request, portfolio evidence, capacity and payment questions; visible contributions from Scout, Operations and Finance.
- **C. Client workspace:** Northstar timeline, English conversation, Indonesian owner recap, scope, next action and team presence.
- **D. Buyer voice page:** Alya's branded service page with a disclosed AI representative, realistic conversation state, transcript and text fallback. It uses the same brand but does not expose the owner's private agent dashboard.
- **E. Offer and payment review:** compare the original request with a reduced two-asset USD 450 offer; show a 40% deposit funding gap and a viable 60% option. Separate owner approval, buyer acceptance and sandbox receipt states.
