# Copy-ready refinement prompts

Use one prompt at a time after the main workspace has generated. Preserve the agreed components and mock content as you refine.

## If the team feels secondary

“Make Principal's six-agent team the main interaction identity. Keep all six distinct cute characters visible in a persistent dock. Show Scout and Promoter working on two separate artifacts with named cursors. Retain the business information and owner decision. Increase the clarity of delegation rather than adding more decorative mascots.”

## If it looks like a generic dashboard

“Replace the wall of KPI cards and charts with a shared work canvas. Make Northstar's request and the community promotional draft the two primary artifacts. Use warm ivory, dark ink, forest-green actions and restrained agent tints. Keep the layout useful and spacious. The main screen should show work being handled.”

## If the characters feel too childish

“Refine the characters into a coherent original vector family with simple rounded silhouettes, dark eyes and small work accessories. Keep their personalities, role labels and distinct shapes. Reduce visual noise and exaggerated expressions. Preserve polished typography and readable business panels.”

## Generate the next screen

“Using the exact visual system and components from the approved workspace, generate Screen B from 02_SCREENS.md. Keep the sidebar, team dock, colors, typography and Northstar fixture consistent. Show the request, portfolio evidence, capacity concern and proposed next action.”

Repeat for C, D and E. The buyer page D uses a simplified owner-branded shell and excludes owner-private business information.

## Add owner control

“Create the selected-agent inspector as a right-side drawer. Show current task, sample sources, action outcomes and output artifact. Include an instruction field, Pause and Cancel. Use Finance and the deposit review as the example. Keep the canvas visible behind it.”

## Make the commercial decision clearer

“Refine Screen E around the reduced two-asset USD 450 offer. Show the difference between 40% and 60% deposits, their estimated net amounts and the upfront expense. Keep gross invoice credit, fees, net receipt and remaining balance separate. Label FX and payments simulated. Owner approval, buyer acceptance and sandbox receipt must have distinct states.”

## Connect a frontend-only walkthrough

“Link the generated screens into a prototype: workspace → opportunity → client → offer review → illustrated sandbox receipt. Agent cards open the inspector. Buyer preview opens the voice page. Use local mock states, with no backend, account setup, microphone access or external service calls. Keep the sample-data label visible.”

## Explore a second direction without losing the idea

“Duplicate the approved workspace as a visual alternative. Keep the same six agents, information hierarchy, named cursors and content. Explore a slightly more editorial composition with stronger typography and fewer panel boundaries. Retain warm light surfaces and forest-green actions. Change visual treatment, not product scope.”

## Mobile adaptation

“Adapt the approved workspace to 390px. Stack the work artifacts, use a compact top bar and four-item bottom navigation, and provide a Team view with six characters in a two-column grid. Keep controls readable and tappable. Do not shrink the whole desktop screen.”

## Final consistency review

“Check all screens for consistent names, package terms, money and state labels. Verify that the six agents remain visible on owner views, private information stays off the buyer page, the deposit never implies completed delivery, and every apparent success has an explicit prototype state. Fix clipped text, low contrast, crowded controls and missing focus states.”
