## 2. Product evolution

The user rejected Clearwork as the overall product because payment administration alone lacked the desired ambition. Its evidence/compatibility/approval/reconciliation mechanisms remain inside Finance. The direction became a freelancer-owned AI agency, then explicitly a business team around professional delivery.

The user introduced cute subagents, numerous cursors and a shared dashboard, inspired by Dot and Google Stitch. Existing-tool coordination drew inspiration from YC Lance. International acquisition, English communication, natural disclosed AI voice, client retention and CRM became central. Informal community promotion and personal service selling expanded the channels beyond Upwork/Fiverr.

A Pareto draft compressed the visible roles into Sales, Client and Finance. The user rejected that reduction. The restored design has Scout, Promoter, Sales, Account Manager, Operations and Finance with genuine task runs. Connector breadth and task depth can be limited; the team identity and broad business coverage remain.

Later research review changed the core decision: specialists should jointly assess acquisition cost/effort, portfolio fit, capacity, terms and collection conditions before commitment. They should verify saved outputs and receipts afterward. The latest request turns this into a self-contained full-app handoff, not another frontend-only exploration.
