# Crewmori skill adapter

This is the active project adaptation for all third-party design/frontend skill text. It resolves the generic prescriptions that would otherwise erase the approved direction. It adds no business scope beyond the final specification.

## Authority and scope

1. Current user instructions and `spec/DECISIONS.md` define the deliverable and product rules.
2. The reconciled project design/brand contracts govern tokens and component behavior. The first two approved Stitch screens govern composition, Newsreader display typography with Inter body/UI text, spacious rhythm, roughly 24 px major panels, the illustrated six-person crew, and Team's Global Studio map. The supplied brand governs logo, geometry and exact palette, including main forest `#245C47`, canvas `#F5F3EC`, surface `#FFFEFA`, ink `#202A25` and muted text `#566159`. Use the project's complete semantic tokens and contrast pairs rather than deriving more colors here.
3. Selected Shopify/Contra references contribute named interaction patterns and information hierarchy. They do not replace Crewmori's visual world or expand it into a marketplace.
4. Third-party skills supply targeted engineering/review advice. Apply their useful checks within the above contract. An upstream aesthetic prohibition or CLI convention cannot override an approved project decision or an environment restriction.

Do not ask the founder to reselect choices already resolved in the pack. Resolve routine implementation choices autonomously, record material assumptions, and follow the pack's rules for any genuinely blocking conflict.

## Verification and truthfulness

Use actual app/browser evidence. Read `VERIFICATION_PLAYBOOK.md` and the complete project acceptance requirements. A screenshot demonstrates appearance for that state and viewport, not the correctness of the interactions behind it. A successful build demonstrates compilation, not production backend readiness or deployment.

Do not hide a required control to avoid implementing it. Each included control must lead to its specified local result, an explicit validation/recovery state, or a clearly explained unavailable state defined by the spec. Never claim sent messages, paid invoices, connected accounts or external publication from a label change alone. All external business behavior is simulated through the shared local service interfaces.

Maintain evidence and technical handoff information in documentation, without cluttering the product UI with implementation prose. Review ratings and critique exchanges stay out of the delivered product and source-facing context narrative unless requested; a concise verification record of what ran and what remains unverified is appropriate.
