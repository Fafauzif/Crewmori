# Assets and upstream notices

The source includes the user-supplied Crewmori brand, illustration and reference package. Original provenance, manifests and licenses are preserved in `docs/brand/original/`, `references/` and `skills/`. Selected reference screenshots and archived documents are research/provenance, not public runtime assets. No ownership claim over third-party reference screenshots is made.

Runtime fonts are local copies supplied in the pack; their license files accompany `public/assets/fonts/`. Dependency versions and resolved packages are in `package-lock.json`; retain their upstream licenses when distributing bundled dependencies.

The documentation snapshots under `skills/vendor/` include upstream notices. They are not runtime services or automatically executed installers. The six original-context archive parts are retained losslessly with their SHA-256 manifest.
